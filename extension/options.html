Let InfoDiet detect and sync the articles you open, on any news site or blog. It runs only on pages that identify themselves as articles (via standard metadata) and reads no article text. Nothing runs on any page until you enable this.

InfoDiet records the article URL and standard page metadata (such as title, description, publisher, publication date, preview image, author, and language) for the article pages you open once capture is enabled. It never collects article text, passwords, or the content of non-article pages, and it does not run on a short list of excluded sites (such as Gmail and Google Docs). Detection activity above is an anonymous local tally (which signal matched / why a page was skipped) that never leaves this browser. Your token is stored only in this browser — revoke it any time from InfoDiet → Settings.
