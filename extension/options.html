InfoDiet — Information Health

Connect this browser to your InfoDiet account so the news articles you read sync automatically.

InfoDiet records the article URL and standard page metadata (such as title, description, publisher, publication date, preview image, author, and language) for supported news sites. It never collects article text, browsing history, passwords, or activity outside supported news sites. Your token is stored only in this browser — revoke it any time from InfoDiet → Settings.
