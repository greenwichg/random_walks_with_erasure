# W8 — Evaluation Plan & Decision Gate (Canonical)

**Status:** Documentation only. This is the **decision authority** for W8 — it determines
whether a behavioral graph should ever replace the synthetic base graph. It supersedes ad-hoc
judgement and sits above the two companion design docs:

- `docs/W8A_BEHAVIORAL_WARM_START_DESIGN.md` — the offline prototype design.
- `docs/W8B_PRODUCTION_BEHAVIORAL_GRAPH_DESIGN.md` — the production migration design.

No production code, no serving change, no schema change, no prototype is created by this
document. Nothing proceeds past a gate below without the specified evidence.

**Established facts (prior audits, this session):**
- The synthetic base graph is generated by `simulate_users.run()` at startup
  (`examples/api_server.py:540-542`), whose clicks are drawn from a utility model that already
  knows each item's gold lean and each user's true viewpoint (`examples/simulate_users.py:6-22`)
  — the **circularity** W8 exists to escape.
- `FeedbackGraph` is built from the click matrix alone (`rwe/graph.py:47-71`); positions/outlets
  enter only in re-ranking.
- MIND item/user IDs do not overlap production; a MIND graph **cannot** transfer — W8 validates
  the **method**, and production must build its own graph from its own reads
  (`docs/W8B_…`, `examples/augmented_corpus.py:148-163`).

---

## 1. Evaluation objective

**What we are trying to prove.** That a **behavioral** recommendation graph — built from real
click co-occurrence, with **no lean labels baked into its generation** — is a credible substrate
for the RWE recommender: at least as good as today's synthetic graph on quality and diversity,
resistant to homogenization, stable, and **fully compatible with the existing explainability
contract**.

**What we are NOT trying to prove.** That MIND beats synthetic on raw accuracy (they are
different datasets; a cross-dataset accuracy number is meaningless — see §3). The synthetic
graph will look *artificially* strong on lean-based metrics precisely because its clicks were
generated from the lean axis; the point is to test the **method on non-circular data**.

**Hypotheses.**

| ID | Hypothesis | Rejected if |
|---|---|---|
| **H1 (non-circularity)** | RWE's diversity / anti-homogenization advantage over baselines survives on real click topology, not just self-generated data | RWE-B/RWE-D lose their diversity edge vs baselines on MIND |
| **H2 (quality)** | RWE stays competitive with strong CF baselines on the behavioral graph, as it does on synthetic | RWE underperforms baselines on accuracy on MIND |
| **H3 (bridging)** | Cross-cutting reach survives on the inferred (unlabelled, sign-arbitrary) axis | RWE-B's `rec_range` collapses to the same region baselines cover |
| **H4 (explainability)** | The REPORT CONTRACT v1 + `rec_explain` parity hold regardless of which graph is the base | Any parity / byte-identity break (§2, metric 8) |
| **H5 (cold-start & stability)** | The behavioral graph degrades gracefully for sparse users and is seed-stable | Cold users get degenerate recs, or top-k is seed-sensitive |

**Null to reject before any production code:** "the behavioral method is no better than, or worse
than, the synthetic graph, and/or homogenizes, and/or breaks explainability."

## 2. Success criteria (objective pass/fail)

All metrics are computed on a held-out split (`rwe/data.py train_test_split`, `test_frac=0.3`)
over `top_k=10` (accuracy) / `diversity_k=20` (diversity), averaged over **≥ 7 seeds** with
paired Wilcoxon significance (`examples/eval_mind.py:94` `_wilcoxon_vs_ref`, `:136`
`_per_user_significance`) — the harness's own robustness recipe. Thresholds below are
**pre-registered targets**, expressed **relative to that dataset's own baselines** (never
cross-dataset), to be confirmed by measurement — not asserted as results.

| # | Metric | Definition | How to compute (code) | Expected | Acceptable (pass) | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Recommendation quality** | Ranking accuracy on held-out clicks | `ndcg_at_k` (`rwe/metrics.py:132`), `hit_rate_at_k` (`:98`), `auc` (`:54`) — RQ2 set (`eval_mind.py:331`) | RWE ≈ best CF baseline on MIND (may trail RP3β slightly) | RWE within noise of the top baseline; not significantly worse (p<0.05) on ndcg@10 | RWE significantly below **all** baselines across ≥7 seeds |
| 2 | **Viewpoint diversity** | Ideological spread of a user's top-k | `rec_range_at_k` (`:213`); long-tail `gini_diversity` (`:148`), `catalog_coverage` (`:163`) — RQ3 (`eval_mind.py:333`) | RWE-B > baselines on `rec_range`, as on synthetic | RWE-B `rec_range@20` significantly > RWE-D & popularity | No significant diversity edge over popularity |
| 3 | **Bridge quality** | Reach into the *opposite* latent region | `directed_shift`/`weighted_shift` (`:281`,`:296`), `rec_range_at_k` — **sign-invariant** (axis unlabelled) | RWE-B reaches distant-region items others don't | RWE-B distant-region reach significantly > RWE-D | Bridge slice collapses to the same region as popularity |
| 4 | **Graph connectivity** | Reachability of the walk substrate | *New:* `scipy.sparse.csgraph.connected_components` over `FeedbackGraph.A_G` (`rwe/graph.py:60`); isolate share via `item_degrees` (`:83`) | One dominant component after k-core | Largest component ≥ 0.90 of nodes; item isolates < 5% (post `_filter_min` 5/5, `rwe/mind.py:140`) | Largest component < 0.70, or > 15% isolates |
| 5 | **Graph density** | Click-matrix fill + degree shape | `A.nnz/(m·n)`; median + Gini of `user_degrees`/`item_degrees` (`rwe/graph.py:78-85`) | Sparse but non-degenerate | Median item degree ≥ 2 after k-core | Median item degree < 2 (no collaborative signal) |
| 6 | **Cold-start behaviour** | Quality vs training-click count | Bucket users (1–2, 3–5, 6–10, >10); accuracy + coverage per bucket | Graceful; cold users fall back to RWE-D popularity | Every bucket non-empty, no errors; monotone-ish improvement | Cold buckets get empty/degenerate lists |
| 7 | **Stability** | Robustness of the axis/recs | *New:* mean top-k **Jaccard** across ≥7 fit seeds/`restarts` (`rwe/ideology.py:138-154`) | High overlap under best-likelihood restarts | Mean Jaccard ≥ 0.70 | Mean Jaccard < 0.50 (axis seed-sensitive) |
| 8 | **Homogenization resistance** | Drift over repeated recommend→click cycles | *New (from W8A):* over T=10 rounds, track `catalog_coverage`, `personalization` (`:176`), `rec_range_at_k` | Flat-or-better than synthetic | Coverage falls ≤ 15% and `personalization` does not collapse over T | Coverage collapses / `personalization`→0 (echo chamber) |
| 9 | **Explainability preservation** | Contract + trace parity intact | REPORT CONTRACT v1 (`rec_sandbox.py:38`, `REPORT_VERSION=1` `:153`), `rec_explain` byte-exact plan replay (`tests/test_rec_explain.py`, `tests/test_rec_sandbox.py`) | Identical regardless of base graph | **All** parity + contract tests pass; explain plan replays byte-exact | **Any** parity/contract break — non-negotiable hard fail |

Metric 9 is a **veto**: an explainability break is an automatic No-Go irrespective of every
other metric, because the product's promise is a transparent, replayable feed.

## 3. Offline experiment design (A / B / C)

**Core principle:** accuracy is compared **within** a dataset against **that dataset's own
baselines** (ItemKNN / BPRMF / P3 / RP3-β + RWE-D/RWE-B, `eval_mind.py:42` `_recommenders`),
never across datasets. The A/B/C comparison is *"does the method retain its properties as the
graph becomes more real?"*

### A. Current synthetic graph (control, known labels)
- Build exactly as production does: `simulate_users.run(cfg, qbias=…)` → `MINDData`
  (`api_server.py:540-542`) → `FeedbackGraph(matrix)` (`rwe/graph.py:34`).
- Run the full battery (§2). This is the **reference column** for every Wilcoxon test and the
  baseline homogenization curve.

### B. MIND behavioural graph (non-circular evidence for H1)
- `python examples/ingest_mind.py --mind-dir <MIND> --political-only --ideology
  --min-user-clicks 5 --min-item-clicks 5 --out mind_ideo.npz` (`examples/ingest_mind.py:97-111`;
  positions via `fit_ideology`, `rwe/mind.py:322`).
- `python examples/eval_mind.py --npz mind_ideo.npz --seeds 7 --out-csv mind.csv` — RQ2/RQ3
  tables + Wilcoxon (`eval_mind.py:330-344`) + axis alignment (`_alignment_report:191`).
- Add the three new metrics (4/7/8) via the W8A prototype script. Report H1–H3, H5.

### C. Future production behavioural graph (transfer evidence)
- Build from real reads with the W8B **offline builder** (store → `from_interactions`
  `rwe/data.py:39` → `FeedbackGraph` → `.npz`), then the identical battery.
- **Live shadow** (no user impact): compute recs from both the synthetic and the behavioral base,
  serve only synthetic, log divergence + metrics. Reuses the deterministic `rec_sandbox.evaluate`
  path (`examples/rec_sandbox.py:399`) and the atomic-swap machinery for staging
  (`examples/corpus_refresh.py`). This is the **only** experiment that proves transfer to the
  real product.

Every experiment is a **standalone offline run**; none imports or mutates a serving path.

## 4. Evaluation datasets

| Dataset | Source (code) | Why it exists | Claims it may support |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Synthetic** | `simulate_users.run` (`api_server.py:540`) | The current Stage-0 base; control with **known** labels + true θ | Reference column; upper bound on lean-metric performance |
| **MIND demo** | `tests/fixtures/mind_demo/` (`news.tsv`+`behaviors.tsv`) | **License-free**, tiny — pipeline smoke-tests + determinism only | Gate 1 (runs + reproducible). **No** statistical claim |
| **MIND full** | Licensed MINDsmall via `ingest_mind.py` | Real, multi-user click topology | H1–H3, H5 (non-circularity, quality, bridging, stability) |
| **Production shadow** | W8B offline builder over `store.Read` (`store.py:249`) | The **actual** future substrate | Transfer (H1–H5 on real product data); Gate 5 |

The demo fixture is for **development and determinism**, never evidence; MIND full is the
**method** evidence; the production shadow is the **substrate** evidence. Confusing "MIND looks
good" with "ship it" is an explicit anti-pattern — MIND cannot be the served graph (§Premise).

## 5. Decision gates (Go / No-Go)

| Gate | Required evidence | Pass criteria | Fail action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1 — Prototype runs** | W8A script executes on the MIND **demo fixture** end-to-end, pinned seed | Deterministic outputs (identical across 2 runs); a valid `FeedbackGraph` + `.npz` | Fix the offline builder; do not touch production; re-run |
| **G2 — Graph quality acceptable** | Metrics 4,5,7 on **MIND full** | Connectivity ≥ 0.90 LCC, isolates < 5%, median item degree ≥ 2, stability Jaccard ≥ 0.70 | Tune k-core / `restarts` / political subset; if still failing → **park** W8 |
| **G3 — Recommendation metrics hold** | Metrics 1,2,3,8 on MIND full, ≥7 seeds, Wilcoxon vs synthetic ref | H1–H3 hold (diversity edge retained, competitive accuracy, bridge survives) **and** homogenization flat-or-better | Diagnose; iterate hyperparameters; if the diversity/anti-homogenization story fails → **No-Go** |
| **G4 — Explainability preserved** | Metric 9: REPORT CONTRACT v1 + `rec_explain` parity suites with a behavioral base | **All** pass; plan replays byte-exact (`tests/test_rec_explain.py`, `tests/test_rec_sandbox.py`) | **Hard stop.** No further stage until parity restored — non-negotiable |
| **G5 — Ready for production shadow** | G1–G4 all green on MIND full **and** enough production reads to build a non-trivial graph (k-core 5/5 on a real cohort) **and** MIND licensing cleared | Repeat G2–G4 on the **production** behavioral graph (dataset C) at parity | Stay in offline evaluation; the synthetic base keeps serving |

Only after **G5** does the W8B rollout (Phase 4–5) begin, and even then behind a flag with
rollback-by-construction (`corpus_refresh.py:16-18`).

## 6. Risks

| Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Monitoring metric |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Sparse graph** | High (early) | High | k-core `_filter_min` (`rwe/mind.py:140`); keep synthetic base until density gate; RWE-D fallback | Metric 5 (density), Metric 6 (cold-start) |
| **Disconnected components** | Med | Med | Serve largest component; `min_item_clicks`; density gate before rollout | Metric 4 (LCC share) |
| **Popularity collapse** (recs converge on head items) | Med | High | RWE-B bridge budget (W1); ε-floor (W2 invariant 3); diversity gate G3 | `gini_diversity` (`:148`), `catalog_coverage` (`:163`) |
| **Feedback loops / homogenization** | Med | High | Homogenization gate (Metric 8) as a hard G3 criterion; independent RWE-B/RWE-D/adaptive slices | Metric 8 curve (coverage / `personalization` over T) |
| **Unstable ideology fit** (non-convex) | Med | Med | `restarts` + best-likelihood selection (`rwe/ideology.py:138-154`); pinned seed; MANIFEST | Metric 7 (stability Jaccard); `lean_corr` |
| **Poor transfer** (MIND ≠ production) | Med | High | Treat MIND as **method** evidence only; require dataset C at G5 | G5 production-shadow deltas |
| **Graph drift** | Med | Med | Batch rebuild cadence (W8B Part 5); pinned, diffable artifacts | Per-rebuild metric deltas |
| **Rebuild cost** (dense fit `O(users×items)`) | Med | Med | `max_cells` guard (`rwe/mind.py:343`); `political_subset`, `sample_users`; walk is cheap sparse | Build wall-time; `max_cells` breaches |
| **Explainability regression** | Low | **Critical** | G4 veto; REPORT CONTRACT v1 byte-identity is a release gate everywhere | Metric 9 (contract + parity suites) |
| **Rollback need** | Low | High | Atomic hot-swap back to synthetic base (`corpus_refresh.py:16-18`); flag-gated | Live health/Open-Mindedness deltas on canary |

## 8. Final recommendation

**Should W8 proceed? Yes — the offline research track only; No production code until the gates
produce evidence.**

The offline track (Phases 1–3) is cheap, fully production-isolated, and answers a real,
non-circular question the synthetic graph structurally cannot. It reuses existing machinery
(`ingest_mind.py`, `eval_mind.py`, `rwe/metrics.py`, `rwe/graph.py`, `rec_sandbox.evaluate`) plus
one new offline script — no serving, schema, or contract exposure. Proceeding is low-risk and
high-information.

**Exact evidence that must exist before a single line of production code is written:**

1. **G1** — the offline prototype runs deterministically on the license-free MIND demo fixture.
2. **G2** — on **MIND full**: connectivity ≥ 0.90 LCC, isolates < 5%, median item degree ≥ 2,
   stability Jaccard ≥ 0.70.
3. **G3** — on MIND full, ≥ 7 seeds with paired Wilcoxon vs the synthetic reference: RWE retains
   its **diversity and bridge advantage** (H1–H3) and the **homogenization curve is flat-or-
   better** than synthetic (Metric 8).
4. **G4 (veto)** — the REPORT CONTRACT v1 byte-identity and `rec_explain` parity suites pass with
   a behavioral base (`tests/test_rec_sandbox.py`, `tests/test_rec_explain.py`).
5. **Preconditions** — MIND licensing cleared; and for anything past Phase 3, **enough production
   reads** to build a non-trivial behavioral graph (k-core 5/5 on a real cohort), because MIND
   validates the *method* but **cannot be the served substrate** (IDs don't transfer).

If G2 or G3 fails after reasonable iteration → **park** W8 and keep the synthetic base (it is
adequate for the current stage; see the rating history in
`docs/PRE_PRODUCTION_RECOMMENDATION_ROADMAP.md`). If G4 ever fails → **hard stop**. Production
code is authorised only when G1–G4 are green on MIND full **and** the Phase-3 production-shadow
(dataset C) reproduces them on real reads.
